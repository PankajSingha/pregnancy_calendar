# Weeks — Pregnancy Calendar

A simple calendar app that highlights each week of pregnancy, counted from your last menstrual period (LMP).

## Features

- Enter your LMP date to start tracking
- Calendar view with weeks highlighted in alternating colors
- Small week number badge on each day
- Summary card showing current week, trimester, estimated due date, and days remaining
- Browse forward/backward through months

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser — no installation or sign-up needed.

To use it like an app on your phone:
1. Open the live site in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android)
2. Tap **Share** (iPhone) or the **⋮ menu** (Android)
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**

## Notes

- Due date is estimated as LMP + 280 days (40 weeks)
- All dates are calculated locally in your browser — nothing is stored or sent anywhere
- This is a planning tool, not medical advice. Confirm dates with your care provider.
